About Local Lift

Your business knowledge.
A clearer way to say it.

Local Lift is built around a simple idea: a content review should leave you with wording you can use, and enough context to decide whether to use it.

The approach

A method you
can follow.

We focus on established, owner-led Perth residential cleaning businesses with an existing website and Google Business Profile. Our work brings selected service descriptions, coverage and enquiry instructions together for a closer look.

The starting point is not an assumption that your content is wrong. It is a set of operating facts you confirm, followed by a comparison with what customers can read now.

Each recommendation explains the issue, why it matters and where the proposed wording belongs. A review call gives you space to question findings, correct details and decide what happens next.

What guides the work

Specific. Transparent.
Owner-confirmed.

Credibility comes from showing the method and its limits. The public sample shows the shape of the work, without presenting fictional examples as results.

01 / Facts first

You know your operation

You confirm services, inclusions, exclusions, suburbs, prices, policies and promises. We do not fill gaps with assumptions or add commitments your business has not made.

02 / Reasons included

You can question the advice

Recommendations come with reasons and priorities. Clear content stays clear. You can accept, adapt or decline a suggestion after discussing it.

03 / Roles made clear

You control publication

The review prepares the changes. You or your web contact publish them. Scope, fees, timing and any separate publishing costs need agreement before work begins.
