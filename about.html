How Local Lift audits your website

We help owner-led Perth residential cleaning businesses work out what to improve on their website, in local search and along the path to a quote.

Business facts come first

You tell us which services you offer, where you work and what you need from a quote request. We compare those details with what customers can read on the agreed pages and public Google Business Profile.

We also check titles, links, basic crawling and indexing signals, and how the site loads and works on a phone. Where a finding needs confirmation from a webmaster or access to Search Console, the handover says who should check it.

Who does what

The audit includes a written handover and a review call. You can ask about a finding, correct details and decide which changes to make.

You confirm the details

Confirm services, inclusions, exclusions, suburbs and any prices, policies or promises used in public copy.

Local Lift prepares recommendations

Get findings with reasons and priorities, specific actions, replacement wording where useful and guidance on where it goes.

You or your web contact publish

You approve the changes and arrange publishing or technical work. Agree the audit scope, fee and timing before work begins.
