What the audit checks

We review agreed pages and public listings, then explain where changes could help people find and use your site.

Service pages and on-page SEO

Check titles, headings, service copy and links so the service, coverage and next step are clear.

Local search and Google Business Profile

Check whether the public profile and website agree on services and the areas you cover.

Basic technical and mobile checks

Look for obvious barriers to crawling and indexing, and check how key pages load and work on a phone.

Enquiry friction

Walk through the quote path to spot unclear instructions, hard-to-find links and questions you can do without.

Who makes the changes?

You get a written list of what to fix first, with suggested wording and where to put it. You decide what to approve, and you or your web contact publish the changes.
