    Five checks for your service area

    Record each mismatch and who can update it as you go.

    1. 1

      Confirm where you work

      Write down the suburbs or areas you currently serve. Note differences by service and any genuine exclusions. Have the owner confirm the list before publishing changes.

    2. 2

      Compare your Google Business Profile

      Check the public service areas and listed services against the confirmed details. Google says service-area businesses that do not serve customers at their address should remove the address from the profile.

      Read Google’s official service-area guidance ↗
    3. 3

      Check the relevant website pages

      Read your homepage, service and coverage pages, footer and anywhere else you name locations. Include page titles and headings if they mention places. Flag old suburb lists and broad statements such as “all of Perth” if they no longer apply.

    4. 4

      Follow the quote-request journey on a phone

      Open the enquiry form. Check suburb choices, prompts and confirmation wording. Make clear what location information to send and when you’ll confirm availability.

    5. 5

      Assign and recheck changes

      Record each mismatch, where it appears, the approved replacement and who will update it. Once published, check the profile and public pages and walk through the quote journey again.

    Updating coverage after a change

    An owner confirms that their standard home cleaning service now covers Maylands and Bayswater. The website still says “all of Perth”, and the quote form lists a suburb they no longer serve.

    Suggested website wording

    “Our standard home cleaning service covers Maylands and Bayswater. Include your suburb in your quote request so we can confirm availability.”
