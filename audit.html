LOCAL LIFT / ILLUSTRATIVE REVIEW

Quote-request guidance

Medium priority
Finding
The page says “Contact us for a quote”, but does not say what information to include.
Why it matters
A visitor has to guess what to send. Explaining the required details makes the next step clearer without promising more enquiries.
Priority reason
The quote link works, so this is not a broken journey. Add the guidance before making smaller wording refinements.
EXACT REPLACEMENT WORDING

“Tell us your suburb, clean type and property size to request a quote.”

Placement
Replace the sentence beside the existing quote link on the agreed service page. Keep the working link.
Responsible publisher
The owner or their web contact. The owner approves the facts before publication.
✓

Already clear. Keep it.

“Oven cleaning is not included in a standard home clean.” If the owner confirms this is accurate, retain it where the inclusions and exclusions are listed. There is no need to rewrite a clear, relevant statement.

Illustrative wording only. Do not copy business details into a real website until the owner has confirmed them.

What does the review cost, and how long does it take?

No fixed fee or turnaround is advertised here. Scope, review fee, timing and the review call arrangements are agreed before work begins. Ask your web contact about any separate publishing costs. An enquiry creates no payment commitment.

What do I need to provide?

Your website, the reason for the review and owner-confirmed operating details. The Google profile link is helpful if you have it. We also need to agree who can publish. No passwords or account access are needed for the initial enquiry.

Will Local Lift make changes for me?

No. The deliverable is a review, prepared copy, placement and responsibility notes, and a review call. You or your web contact handle publication and any related technical work.

What is excluded?

Implementation, comprehensive SEO, technical audits, rebuilds, full-site rewriting, ongoing management, advertising and account management are outside scope. Rankings, enquiries, conversions and savings are not promised.
