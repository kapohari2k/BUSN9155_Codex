Clarify the service page title

Fictional example · Regular house cleaning page · On-page SEO

Medium priority
Finding
The title uses “Services” as its only description. The owner has confirmed that this page is about regular house cleaning in Maylands, but neither detail appears in the title.
Why it matters
A descriptive title helps people recognise a relevant result and helps search engines understand the page. Google may display a different title in search.

Page title before

Services | Sample Cleaning

Recommended page title

Regular House Cleaning in Maylands | Sample Cleaning

Change and placement
Update the SEO page title for the existing regular house cleaning page. Keep its current URL. After publishing, open the public page and check its browser tab title.
Who acts
The owner confirms the service and suburb. The owner or their web contact updates the title, publishes it and checks the live page.

Priority rationale · Improve this main service page after fixing any issues that prevent customers or search engines from reaching it.

Four areas we check

We record what we observe, why it matters and what to do next. If a technical issue needs further investigation, the handover identifies the next check and who can do it.

On-page SEO

Review page titles, descriptions, headings, service copy and internal links on agreed pages. Flag unclear or repeated text and suggest specific edits where they help explain your services and coverage.

Local search and Google Business Profile

Compare the public profile’s services, service areas and website link with the website and owner-confirmed details. If you have no profile, flag whether checking Google’s eligibility guidance is a sensible next step.

Crawling, indexing and mobile performance

Check key public URLs for broken links, redirects, sitemap coverage, crawl restrictions and signals such as noindex or canonical tags. Spot-check phone layout and loading, including large images. Confirming Google’s indexing status may require your Search Console access.

Enquiry friction

Follow the quote path on desktop and phone. Check whether the next step is easy to find, instructions are clear and the form asks for the right information. Recommend changes to links, wording or fields as needed.
